# <Short name for this story>

One file per story, at `profile/accomplishments/<slug>.md`. STAR format. Tag it
with the skills it demonstrates so `interview-drill` and resume prep can find it.

## Situation

The context and the stakes. What was going on, why it mattered.

## Task

What was actually asked of you. The specific problem you owned.

## Action

What YOU did, step by step. Not "we". Pin down your own contribution. Include the
technical detail: how you built it, what you chose, what you rejected.

## Result

The outcome, with a number where one exists. If you cannot quantify it, say what
would have happened without you.

## Reflection

What was hard, what you would do differently, what you learned. This is the
material that separates a strong answer from a rehearsed one.
